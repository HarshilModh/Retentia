# Chromosomes: The Blueprints of Life

Chromosomes are thread-like structures located inside the nucleus of animal and plant cells. Each chromosome is made of protein and a single molecule of deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA). Passed from parents to offspring, DNA contains the specific instructions that make each type of living creature unique.

## Structure of Chromosomes
A chromosome has a highly organized structure:
1. **DNA and Histones**: DNA wraps tightly around proteins called histones. This packaging allows extremely long DNA molecules to fit inside the tiny cell nucleus.
2. **Chromatids**: During cell division, chromosomes replicate. The two identical copies are called sister chromatids.
3. **Centromere**: The sister chromatids are joined at a constricted region called the centromere. The centromere divides the chromosome into a short arm (p arm) and a long arm (q arm).

## Human Chromosomes
Humans have 23 pairs of chromosomes, for a total of 46 chromosomes in each cell:
- **Autosomes**: 22 pairs are homologous (non-sex) chromosomes, which look identical in males and females.
- **Sex Chromosomes**: The 23rd pair determines the biological sex (XX for females, XY for males).

## Importance of Chromosomes
Chromosomes ensure that DNA is accurately copied and distributed during cell division. Any alteration in the number or structure of chromosomes can lead to serious genetic disorders, such as Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21).
